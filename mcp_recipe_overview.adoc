== Standard investigation pattern

. Clarify the resource scope and time range.
. Discover available types.
. Retrieve metadata before using dynamic fields.
. Query a bounded time window.
. Correlate independent signals.
. Distinguish observed facts from inferences.
. Report limitations and recommended follow-up.

== Safety

* These recipes use read-only tools.
* Keep time windows to 30 days or less.
* Omit credentials, private addresses, UUIDs, raw webhook endpoints, and unnecessary log payloads.
* Confirm high-impact operational actions outside the MCP workflow.
